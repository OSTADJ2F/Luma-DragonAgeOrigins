"The Fade has never been this bright."

**Luma: Dragon Age: Origins** (work in progress)

• Real HDR (and a real SDR mode)
• Recovered highlights, with their colour (sky, sun, fire)
• Luma TAA, DLSS and FSR anti-aliasing
• Bloom: Luma, the game's own, or off
• Film Grain
• Ambient Occlusion (XeGTAO or GT-VBAO)
• HDR-aware MSAA resolve and SMAA
• Anisotropic Filtering
• HUD Scale (Auto by default: the HUD, menus and click targets at the size a modern screen deserves)
• Vignette and chromatic aberration
• Wide color gamut
• AutoHDR for the videos
• Hide the HUD for screenshots

**HUD Scale is "Auto" by default**, which is the size the game's interface has at 768 lines: about 1.4x at 1080p and 1.9x at 1440p, with the mouse and click targets on the same scale. Set it to 1.0 for the game's own size.
