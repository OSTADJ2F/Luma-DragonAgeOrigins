=== SUMMARY (Nexus "About this mod" field, paste separately) ===
Native HDR for Dragon Age: Origins (and an SDR mode), with DLSS or FSR anti-aliasing, a new bloom, cinematic depth of field in cutscenes, ambient occlusion, film grain, a HUD sized for modern displays and more. A ReShade addon, not a preset.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Real HDR[/b] for a game that only ever had standard (SDR) output, with Paper White and Peak White settings for your display.
[*][b]Works in SDR too:[/b] with HDR off in Windows the mod outputs real SDR, and the anti-aliasing, bloom, ambient occlusion, depth of field and film grain still work.
[*][b]Bright things look bright:[/b] skies, the sun, fire and magic keep their detail and colour instead of washing out to flat white, and the brightest parts use your display's full brightness. The highlights adapt to your Peak White and Paper White by themselves.
[*][b]Better anti-aliasing:[/b] choose [b]DLSS[/b] (NVIDIA RTX) or [b]FSR[/b] (any graphics card) instead of the game's MSAA, for cleaner edges and a steadier image in motion.
[*][b]New bloom:[/b] a softer, wider glow around bright lights, or keep the game's own bloom, or turn bloom off.
[*][b]Cinematic depth of field[/b] in cutscenes: out of focus areas blur into soft discs and bright lights turn into bokeh.
[*][b]Ambient occlusion:[/b] soft shadowing in corners, creases and where objects meet, which the game never had. Choose [b]XeGTAO[/b] (crisp) or [b]GT-VBAO[/b] (softer).
[*][b]Film grain:[/b] an optional film look, stronger on brighter areas.
[*][b]Adjustable UI brightness:[/b] set how bright the HUD and menus are, separately from the game.
[*][b]A HUD that fits a modern screen:[/b] the game draws its interface for 1024x768, so it stays small on a 1080p or 1440p display. [b]HUD Scale[/b] enlarges the HUD, menus and the character sheet together (the mouse and every click target follow the same scale, so nothing drifts). [b]"Auto" is the default[/b]: the size the HUD has at 768 lines, about 1.4x at 1080p and 1.9x at 1440p. Any size from 0.5x to 3x can be picked, and 1.0 is the game's own.
[*][b]More detail in the dark:[/b] a shadow lift that brings back detail in the darkest areas.
[*][b]Natural highlights:[/b] the very brightest lights fade towards white, like on film, instead of staying a vivid colour.
[*][b]Richer colours:[/b] the game's colours use the wider range of an HDR display.
[*][b]HDR for the movies:[/b] a light brightness boost for the pre-rendered videos.
[*][b]Sharper textures:[/b] 16x anisotropic filtering, always on.
[*][b]Sharpening:[/b] a slider for the upscalers, off by default.
[*][b]Colour controls:[/b] Exposure, Contrast, Saturation and Temperature sliders.
[*][b]Lens effects:[/b] an optional vignette (with a radius slider) that darkens the corners of the scene and not the HUD, and chromatic aberration for a slight film-like fringe towards the edges.
[*][b]Hide the HUD[/b] for clean screenshots.
[*][b]Borderless window[/b] support.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]For HDR: an HDR display with HDR turned on in Windows. With HDR off the mod runs in SDR. It picks the mode when the game starts: restart the game after turning Windows HDR on or off.
[*][b]Frame Buffer Effects turned on[/b] in the game's video settings. The mod does not work without it.
[*]For [b]DLSS or FSR: MSAA turned off[/b] in the game's video settings. Ambient occlusion and bloom work either way.
[*]DLSS needs an NVIDIA RTX graphics card. FSR runs on any graphics card.
[*]TAA and SMAA (the mod's own temporal anti-aliasing and the old SMAA path) and XeSS are not part of this release.
[/list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Extract everything (all the files [b]and[/b] the "Luma" folder) next to the game's exe, in the [b]bin_ship[/b] folder. Back up D3D9.dll, dxgi.dll and dgVoodoo.conf first if it asks to replace them.
[*]For HDR, turn on HDR in Windows. Start the game and press [b]HOME[/b] to open the Luma tab.
[*]In HDR, set Scene Paper White, UI Paper White and Peak White for your display. Then pick a [b]Super Resolution[/b] option (None keeps the game's own anti-aliasing).
[/list]
Keep all the included files together in that folder, and do not swap in a different version of dgVoodoo: the mod only works with the one it comes with.

[size=4][b]Settings (Luma tab)[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Scene Paper White[/b] (102 by default) and [b]UI Paper White[/b] (203 by default): how bright the game and the HUD are.
[*][b]Peak White[/b]: your display's peak brightness, as Windows reports it.
[*][b]Super Resolution:[/b] None, Auto, DLSS or FSR. A tick appears when it is running. DLSS and FSR can run through Direct3D 11 or 12 (the D3D12 path gives FSR 4 on the newest AMD cards, FSR 3.1 everywhere else); DLSS and FSR 3 look the same either way.
[*][b]Highlight Desaturation[/b] (0, HDR only): how much the brightest lights fade towards white. 0 keeps their colour.
[*][b]Exposure, Contrast, Saturation, Temperature:[/b] the usual colour sliders, neutral by default.
[*][b]Bloom[/b] (Luma): Off, Game (the game's own bloom) or Luma (a wider, softer bloom), and [b]Bloom Intensity[/b] (1.0, for Luma). For Game and Luma keep the game's own Bloom option turned on.
[*][b]Film Grain[/b] (0.35): 0 turns it off.
[*][b]Vignette[/b] (off) with [b]Vignette Radius[/b], and [b]Chromatic Aberration[/b] (off): the lens effects, both off by default.
[*][b]Bokeh Depth of Field[/b] (on): turn it off for the game's own blur.
[*][b]Video AutoHDR[/b] (on) and [b]Video HDR Boost[/b] (0.5, HDR only): the brightness boost for the movies.
[*][b]RCAS Sharpness[/b] (0.25, for the upscalers, 0 is off).
[*][b]HUD Scale[/b] (Auto): 0 (Auto) is the size the HUD has at 768 lines, 1.0 is the game's own size, higher is bigger. The menus, the character sheet, the mouse and every click target follow it. It applies the next time the game lays its interface out (change screen, or restart to be sure).
[*][b]Hide Gameplay UI[/b] (off, not saved): hides the HUD for clean screenshots.
[*][b]Ambient Occlusion[/b] (on) and [b]AO Technique[/b] (GT-VBAO by default, or XeGTAO).
[/list]

[size=4][b]FAQ[/b][/size]
[b]Nothing seems to happen.[/b] Check that Frame Buffer Effects is on in the game's video settings, and for HDR that HDR is on in Windows (then restart the game).
[b]There are no Paper White or Peak White settings.[/b] The mod is in SDR because Windows HDR was off when the game started. Turn it on and restart the game.
[b]Bloom Off, Game and Luma look almost the same in SDR.[/b] The game's bloom lands mostly on areas that are already white in SDR. The difference shows in HDR.
[b]DLSS or FSR is greyed out, or shows no tick.[/b] Turn MSAA off in the game's video settings, and make sure [b]Luma-Upscaler.dll[/b] (the small helper the mod starts for DLSS and FSR) is in the bin_ship folder: some antivirus tools remove it. It can take a few seconds after you pick DLSS or FSR.
[b]The highlights look limited.[/b] Peak White should match your display's real peak brightness, and be higher than Paper White.
[b]The HUD is too bright or too dim.[/b] Adjust UI Paper White.
[b]The HUD is bigger than it used to be.[/b] That is [b]HUD Scale[/b] on [b]Auto[/b] (the default): the game's interface was drawn for 1024x768, so it stays small on a 1080p or 1440p screen. Set HUD Scale to 1.0 for the game's own size.
[b]The HUD looks blurry when it is big.[/b] The game's interface art has a fixed resolution, so it is magnified along with the HUD. Lower HUD Scale if it bothers you.
[b]The bloom, ambient occlusion or film grain is too strong or too weak.[/b] Adjust its slider, or turn it off.
[b]Can I use it with another HDR mod?[/b] No, do not run another HDR mod (for example RenoDX) alongside it.
[b]Some skies show banding.[/b] That comes from the game's own art.

[size=4][b]Known issues[/b][/size]

[*]DLSS and FSR smooth the image at the game's own resolution (they do not upscale it).

[/list]
